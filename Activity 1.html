Diary of a Wimpy Kid

Greg Heffley begins middle school and keeps a journal about his daily adventures. He hopes to become popular, but school, friendships, and family life keep getting in the way. With funny observations and cartoon drawings, the first book follows Greg as he tries to handle the awkward moments of growing up.

Diary of a Wimpy Kid: Rodrick Rules

Greg's older brother Rodrick knows a secret Greg wants to keep hidden and uses it to tease him. Between school, family rules, and Rodrick's band, Greg finds himself in one awkward situation after another. This story focuses on sibling rivalry and the trouble the brothers get into together.

Diary of a Wimpy Kid: The Last Straw

Greg's dad thinks he should become tougher and more responsible, so he encourages Greg to try new activities. Greg would rather stick to his own comfortable routine, which leads to comic disagreements and mishaps. The third book follows Greg as he tries to meet his dad's expectations in his own way.
